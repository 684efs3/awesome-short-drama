# Awesome Short Drama [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of resources, apps, tools, and communities for short drama (micro-drama) enthusiasts and industry professionals.

Short dramas (短剧) are vertical-format episodic series, typically 1-3 minutes per episode with 60-100+ episodes per season. The industry has grown to over $26 billion globally.

## Platforms & Apps

### Global / English
- [ReelShort](https://www.reelshort.com) - The largest English-language short drama app by Crazy Maple Studio.
- [DramaBox](https://www.dramaboxapp.com) - Popular short drama platform with multi-language subtitles.
- [ShortMax](https://www.shortmax.com) - Short drama app with curated content library.
- [FlexTV](https://www.flextv.co) - Vertical drama platform with diverse content.
- [MoboReels](https://www.moboreels.com) - Free-to-watch short drama app with ad-supported model.

### Chinese Language
- [红果短剧 (Hongguo)](https://hongguoduanju.com) - ByteDance's free short drama platform. Available in CN/HK/TW/MY/SG/Macau.
- [番茄小说短剧频道 (Fanqie)](https://fanqieduanju.com) - ByteDance's novel + short drama platform. Globally available.
- [抖音短剧 (Douyin)](https://www.douyin.com) - Short drama channel within Douyin. Mainland China & Hong Kong only.
- [爱奇艺短剧 (iQIYI)](https://www.iqiyi.com) - Baidu's video platform with dedicated short drama section. Global as "iQIYI".
- [腾讯视频短剧 (WeTV)](https://v.qq.com) - Tencent's video platform. Global as "WeTV".
- [PineDrama](https://www.pinedrama.com) - TikTok/ByteDance's overseas short drama platform.

### Southeast Asia
- [NetShort](https://www.netshort.com) - Singapore-based short drama platform.
- [Do Drama](https://www.dodrama.com) - Malaysia-focused short drama app.
